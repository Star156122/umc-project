Hybrid Risk Management V1

此結果仍為 2023–2024 Training OOF 研究階段，不是 Final OOS，也不是投資建議。

Risk V1 定義與公式

risk_per_trade = 1%；max_position_fraction = 40%；沿用 Technical V1 Stop Loss = 2.5%。

risk_budget = current_equity × 0.01
stop_distance_per_share = entry_price × 0.025
quantity_by_risk = floor(risk_budget / stop_distance_per_share)
quantity_by_exposure = floor(current_equity × 0.40 / entry_price)
final_quantity = min(quantity_by_risk, quantity_by_exposure, existing_engine_buying_limit)

Risk Manager 僅在 ML BUY gate 通過後計算股數；Hold/Sell 不進 sizing。
